Our Story

DEPARTURZ was born from a simple belief: every project deserves the same level of craft, whether it's a major television production or an independent passion project. We don't cut corners. We craft departures from the ordinary.

Founded by Nathan Olu, DEPARTURZ brings together professional post-production and creative production services under one roof. With experience across television, commercial, travel, and branded content, we deliver work that demands attention and respects the viewer's time.

We're based in Los Angeles and Houston, working with clients across the country. Whether you need a full production crew or a dedicated editor, we bring the same level of professionalism and creative intensity to every engagement.
