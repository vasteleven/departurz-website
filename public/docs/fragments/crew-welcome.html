Welcome to the DEPARTURZ crew. I'm glad to have you with us. This letter covers how we work, how you get paid, and the few rules that matter most.

Your first week

  1. Sign in at departurz.com/portal with [your email]. You'll get a one-time sign-in link by email, no password.
  2. Sign the Crew Agreement under Documents.
  3. Send your W-9 through the secure link I'll text you. Please never email tax forms.
  4. Fill in your profile: your payout method (Zelle, PayPal, Venmo or direct deposit) and your crew card.
  5. Say hello in #general.

How jobs work

  • Every job comes to you as a booking with a code name, dates, call time, location, role, rate and deadline. Accept it or pass. Both are fine.
  • Tasks, files, cuts and messages for the job all live in the portal.
  • Cuts you finish go to me first in the Editors room. Nothing goes to a client until I've reviewed it.
  • On shoot days, be set up and ready to roll at call time.

Getting paid

  • Log your hours and finished work in the portal within three business days of wrapping.
  • Approved payouts go out within fifteen days to the method in your profile.
  • Expenses need my OK before you spend, and a receipt after.

The house rules

  • Code names only. Projects come to you under code names. Keep real names out of file names, posts and conversations outside the job.
  • No direct client contact. If a client reaches out to you, on set or after, point them to me. That protects you, the client and the work.
  • Nothing goes public until it's public. No behind-the-scenes posts, clips or stills until the project is out and I've said yes. Then post away and tag us.
  • Your login is yours. Don't share it.
  • Respect on set. We look out for each other, every day, every role.

Who to call

Me, for anything: wecreate@departurz.com, 832-837-0772, or a message in the portal.

Glad you're here. Let's make some good work.
