1. Working relationship

1.1 Crew Member is an independent contractor running their own business, not an employee, agent or partner of DEPARTURZ. Crew Member decides how to do the work, uses their own judgment and skills, and may work for others, including other production companies.

1.2 Crew Member is responsible for their own taxes, licenses and business expenses, and receives no employee benefits. Before the first payment, Crew Member provides a completed Form W-9 through the secure link DEPARTURZ sends. DEPARTURZ issues a Form 1099-NEC where the law requires it.

1.3 The parties intend this to be an independent contractor relationship under California law, including the ABC test. If a change in the law or official guidance requires a different arrangement, both parties will work in good faith to restructure it while keeping the economic terms as close as possible.

2. Bookings

2.1 Each job is offered as a booking in the DEPARTURZ portal or in writing, with the project's code name, the dates, call time, location, role, rate, deliverables and deadline. Crew Member may accept or decline any booking.

2.2 An accepted booking is a commitment. If Crew Member has to cancel, they tell Nate as early as possible, and at least [forty-eight (48)] hours before call, except in an emergency.

2.3 If DEPARTURZ cancels a booked day less than [forty-eight (48)] hours before call, it pays [fifty percent (50%)] of that day's rate, and the full day rate if cancelled after call.

3. Pay

3.1 DEPARTURZ pays the rate in each booking, or the standard rate in the details above if a booking does not state one.

3.2 Crew Member logs hours and completed work in the portal within [three (3)] business days of finishing. DEPARTURZ pays approved amounts within [fifteen (15)] days, to the payout method in Crew Member's portal profile. Payouts are not delayed for a late client payment.

3.3 On shoot days, the standard day is ten (10) hours including a thirty (30) minute meal. Hours 11 and 12 are paid at 1.5x the hourly equivalent of the day rate, and hours 13 and later at 2x, when the overtime was approved by DEPARTURZ.

3.4 Expenses are reimbursed only when approved in advance, with receipts, and are paid with the next payout.

4. Equipment

4.1 Crew Member brings their own tools and equipment unless the booking says DEPARTURZ provides them, and is responsible for their own gear and its insurance.

4.2 Crew Member takes reasonable care of any DEPARTURZ or rented equipment, reports any damage or loss right away, and returns it on time. Crew Member is responsible for damage or loss caused by their negligence or misuse.

5. Clients and code names

5.1 DEPARTURZ keeps its crew and its clients separate. Projects are shared with crew under code names, and client names and details are shared only when the job needs them.

5.2 Crew Member does not contact DEPARTURZ clients directly, before, during or after a job, except as DEPARTURZ directs on set. Any question, request or offer from a client goes to Nate.

5.3 Crew Member does not use confidential information gained through DEPARTURZ, including client identities, contacts, rates or project details, to solicit DEPARTURZ clients or to compete for their work.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 Everything Crew Member learns through DEPARTURZ is confidential: client identities, project code names and real titles, footage, scripts, designs, schedules, rates, the portal and its contents, passwords, and DEPARTURZ's business information.

6.2 Crew Member does not share or post any of it, including behind-the-scenes photos or clips, without written approval from DEPARTURZ, and keeps files only as long as the job needs them.

6.3 Crew Member keeps their portal login private and never shares it.

6.4 These obligations continue after this Agreement ends, for as long as the information stays confidential.

7. Work product

7.1 All work Crew Member creates for DEPARTURZ projects, including footage, edits, audio, designs, code, notes and project files, belongs to DEPARTURZ or its client. Crew Member assigns all rights in that work to DEPARTURZ, and signs any document needed to confirm it.

7.2 Crew Member keeps ownership of their own pre-existing tools, presets and know-how.

7.3 Crew Member may show work in their reel or portfolio only after the project is public, and only with written approval from DEPARTURZ. Credits follow the booking.

8. Conduct and safety

8.1 Crew Member arrives on time, prepared, and ready to work, and treats everyone on set and online with respect. DEPARTURZ does not tolerate harassment, discrimination, or working under the influence of alcohol or drugs.

8.2 Crew Member follows safety directions on set, and may decline any task they consider unsafe. Drone work is flown only by a crew member with a current FAA Part 107 certificate.

9. Ending the agreement

9.1 Either party may end this Agreement at any time with written notice. Accepted bookings are completed or handed off as agreed.

9.2 When the Agreement ends, Crew Member returns any DEPARTURZ equipment and materials, deletes project files they no longer need, and is paid for approved work.

10. Liability

Each party is responsible for its own negligence and misconduct. Crew Member will defend and hold DEPARTURZ harmless from claims caused by Crew Member's breach of this Agreement, negligence, or misuse of confidential information. Neither party is liable to the other for indirect or consequential damages.

11. General terms

11.1 California law governs this Agreement. Disputes are first discussed in good faith, then taken to mediation in Los Angeles County, California, and then to the courts there.

11.2 This Agreement and each booking are the full agreement between the parties. Changes must be in writing and confirmed by both parties; email and portal messages count.

11.3 Accepting this Agreement in the DEPARTURZ portal, or signing it electronically, has the same effect as a handwritten signature.

11.4 If any part of this Agreement cannot be enforced, the rest stays in effect. Sections 5, 6, 7 and 10 continue after the Agreement ends.
