Portal setup

This connects the portal to the departurzedits@gmail.com Drive and builds its home there: the folders, the Clients and Team sheets, and the books. It takes about a minute, and it's safe to run again.

  1. 1Google sign-in keyGOOGLE_CLIENT_ID and GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET in Vercel
  2. 2DatabaseUpstash for Redis, connected under Vercel > Storage
  3. 3Setup keySETUP_KEY in Vercel: a long phrase only you know
  4. 4Daily jobs keyCRON_SECRET in Vercel: another long phrase (it unlocks the morning emails)
  5. 5EmailRESEND_API_KEY in Vercel, for sign-in links and notifications (can come after)
  6. 6Drive connectedThis page does it

Google asks you to sign in: pick departurzedits@gmail.com. It may say it hasn't verified the app. That's expected for your own private app: click Advanced, then Go to DEPARTURZ, then Continue, and allow Drive and Sheets.

The portal is already connected. Running this again only adds anything that's missing; nothing is erased. Open the portal.
