Privacy

Last updated November 5, 2026

This explains what DEPARTURZ collects through departurz.com and the client and crew portal, why, and the choices you have. We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use advertising trackers.

What we collect

When you contact us: your name, email, company, the services you're interested in, dates, budget and anything you write about your project.

In the portal: your name and email (from Google if you sign in with Google), plus what you choose to add: company, role, city, bio, social handles and email preferences. Also the messages, files, notes, bookings and calendar items you send, and a record of documents you accept (your typed name, email and the time). Crew members also add a payout method with an account email or the last four digits only, never full account numbers, and the hours they submit.

Automatically: basic technical information such as your IP address, browser and the pages you visit, kept in our hosting provider's logs. The portal uses one cookie to keep you signed in. We don't use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Payments: handled entirely by Stripe, PayPal or Square on their own secure pages. We receive confirmation that you paid, not your card or bank details.

How we use it

To reply to you, plan and deliver your project, run the portal, send project emails (reminders, updates, cuts ready for review, receipts), keep the Site secure, meet legal and accounting duties, and, if you leave them switched on, send thank-you notes and returning-client offers.

Google sign-in and Google user data

Signing in with Google: if you choose Continue with Google on the portal sign-in page, Google shares your basic profile with us: your name, email address and profile picture. We use your email to confirm who you are and open your portal, and your name to address you. We don't ask for access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or calendar.

The studio's own Google Drive: DEPARTURZ connects the portal to its own Google account (the studio's, not yours) so that project files, cuts for review and the records behind the portal are kept in the studio's Google Drive and Google Sheets. That access is used only to run the portal for our clients and crew.

Information we receive from Google APIs is used only to provide and improve the portal. We don't sell it, don't use it for advertising, don't use it to train AI or machine learning models, and don't share it with anyone except the services listed below that help run the portal, or when the law requires it. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can remove the portal's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and ask us to delete what we hold through the contact page, or by mail at DEPARTURZ, 3311 La Cienega Blvd., Los Angeles, CA 90016.

Who helps us

We share information only with the services that run the Site for us, and only what each one needs:

  • Google (sign-in, and the Drive and Sheets where portal files and records are kept)
  • Vercel (hosting) and Upstash (portal messages)
  • Resend (sending email) and Kit (project updates, thank-yous and offers)
  • Stripe, PayPal and Square (payments)
  • Anthropic (to answer questions typed into the chat assistant)
  • Google Fonts and Cloudflare (fonts and code libraries the pages load)

We may also share information if the law requires it, if someone breaks our Terms of Use, to protect our rights or someone's safety, or as part of a sale or merger of the business.

Payment pages, social networks and the other sites we link to have their own privacy policies, and those apply once you're there.

Do Not Track

We don't sell or share personal information, don't use cross-site tracking, and don't let ad networks follow you on our pages. Browser signals like Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are welcome, and the Site already works the way they ask.

Your choices

You can change your email preferences on your Profile page in the portal or with the unsubscribe link in any email, and you can withdraw your consent to marketing emails at any time. Project emails about work in progress keep coming while a project is active. You can ask to see, correct or delete your information through the contact page, or by mail at DEPARTURZ, 3311 La Cienega Blvd., Los Angeles, CA 90016. Depending on where you live, including California and Texas, you may have these rights by law, and we won't treat you differently for using them.

How long we keep it

We keep information for as long as your project and our working relationship need it, then as long as the law requires for tax and accounting records. Portal chat keeps the most recent 500 messages in each conversation.

Security

We use secure connections, sign-in links that work once and expire, and access limited to the people working on your project. No system is perfectly secure, so please tell us right away if you think something is wrong.

Watch for impersonators

DEPARTURZ will never ask you to pay to be considered for a role or a job, and will never ask for your password or full bank or card details by text, email or direct message. Invoices only come through your portal or from us directly. If someone claiming to be DEPARTURZ asks for any of that, it isn't us; please tell us through the contact page.

If you're in our footage

We film events, shoots and behind-the-scenes moments, and the people in them may appear in work we publish. If you're in something we made and have a concern, contact us and we'll review it with you.

Children

The Site isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Visitors and crew outside the U.S.

Information is stored and processed in the United States. If you use the Site or the portal from another country, your information will be handled there.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll let portal users know by email.

Questions

Reach us through the contact page, or by mail at DEPARTURZ, 3311 La Cienega Blvd., Los Angeles, CA 90016.
